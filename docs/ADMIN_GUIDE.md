# Administrator Guide

---

## 1. System Overview
The AIUB Job Fair 2026 Admin Portal is accessible to users with the `Admin` role at `/admin`.

---

## 2. Key Administrative Responsibilities

### 2.1 Employer Approvals
1. When an employer registers, their company status is set to `Pending`.
2. Admin reviews the company details (Name, HR Contact, Website, Logo, Short Intro).
3. Admin clicks **Approve** or **Reject**.
4. Once approved, the company appears on the public "Participating Companies" page and can publish job openings or desired candidate criteria.

### 2.2 Student & Application Oversight
* Admin can search, filter, and export the student directory and applications to CSV format.
* Admin can view student progress and confirm completion of required profile sections and CV upload.

### 2.3 Seminars & Workshops Management
* Admin creates seminars (Title, Description, Speaker/Venue, StartsAtUtc, Capacity).
* Students opt-in during or after registration.

### 2.4 Psychometric Assessment Review Note (Section 7A)
* The platform includes a standardized Big Five self-assessment fallback from the International Personality Item Pool (IPIP).
* **Important:** AIUB's career/counselling office or a qualified faculty member should review the questions and scoring bands before launch. Any question updates can be imported via CSV directly into the admin question bank with zero code changes.
